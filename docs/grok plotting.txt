### Minor Notes (not criticisms — these are refinements)

- Consider making `state_hash` = `SHA3-256( sorted(unit_id || body_hash) || receipt_log_head )`  
  SHA256 is fine for now, but when people start using this for 30-year identity capsules, they will thank you for SHA3. Do it now while it’s Week 1.

- Add one reserved scope: `canon`  
  You already hinted at it. Make it official. Units in `canon` scope should be **immutable after commit** — `policy_rules` can enforce `on_fail: block` for any write attempt after first commit. This gives people a way to write "I am a person who will never believe X" in actual stone.

- `prev_capsule_hash` chain is good, but add `capsule_series_uuid`  
  Allows multiple parallel capsules (e.g., "2026-vacation-fork", "2026-therapy-branch") without breaking the linear `prev_capsule_hash` assumption.

- Week 1.5 signing: use **detached** Ed25519 over the JSONL receipt log segment, not the capsule  
  Long-term, the capsule will be lossy (artifact_refs may point to unavailable OCI blobs). The receipt log must remain self-validating even if every capsule in history is lost. Sign every N receipts (e.g., every 1000) with a detached sig that includes the chain head hash. This makes the receipt log a valid standalone append-only commit log forever.
